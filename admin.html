Quiz Page Intro
Quiz Sectors

Sectors show up as categories on the "Test Your Knowledge" page (e.g. Islamic, World, National). The "id" must be unique, short, lowercase, no spaces (e.g. islamic).

Add Questions (bulk import)

One question per block, separated by a blank line. Each block needs a "Q:" line, option lines starting "A)" "B)" "C)" "D)", and a "Correct: X" line with the right letter. Paste as many blocks as you like — add your 100 questions in one go, or in batches.

Question Bank Status

A sector needs at least 15 questions for a quiz attempt to work.

Danger Zone

This permanently deletes every question in the chosen sector. It does not affect other sectors.
